# Moonshine Run privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Moonshine Run is a free car-chase game by Benjamin Rahtkens for Android (and later iPhone). This policy covers the game and this website. Contact: moonshine-run@proton.me

## The short version

The game has no internet access. It sends nothing to me or anyone else: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services.

## What stays on your device

Scores, settings, garage progress and replays are saved on your phone only. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Tilt steering reads the motion sensor while you play; the readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent.

## Optional feedback reports

If you ask it to, the game creates a feedback or bug report file. You see the whole report before you save it. Nothing is sent automatically: a report reaches me only if you email the file yourself to moonshine-run@proton.me.

A report contains your answers, the game version and, only if you switch each one on, usage totals (how long and how you play), frame-rate totals and a rough description of your device. Linked reports share a random ID, not your name. Sending an email shows me your email address.

I use reports only to fix bugs and improve the game, with your consent. Raw reports and emails are deleted within 30 days and tuning totals within 90 days. Email is delivered and stored by Proton AG (Switzerland). Reports are never sold or used for advertising.

## Children

Moonshine Run is not made for children under 13, and I do not knowingly receive their data.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of, a correction to or the deletion of any report you sent, or withdraw your consent, by emailing moonshine-run@proton.me with the subject "Privacy request". I reply within one month. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party fonts. GitHub, as the host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the page and keep it secure; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, I update this page before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
