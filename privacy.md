# Moonshine Run privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Moonshine Run is a free car-chase game by Benjamin Rahtkens for Android (and later iPhone). This policy covers the game and this website. Benjamin Rahtkens is responsible for the data described here. Contact: moonshine-run@proton.me

## The short version

The game has no internet access and sends nothing automatically: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. Data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share it yourself.

## What the game keeps on your device

Scores, statistics, settings, garage progress, saved setups and replays are stored inside the game on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Tilt steering reads the motion sensor while you play; the readings are used on the spot and are never stored or sent.

## What you can choose to export

Some features create a file you save to a place you choose, or text you copy:

- **Setups, runs, replays and challenges.** Sharing one creates a file or text containing the setup (and any name you gave it), the run's seed, your driving and its score.
- **Feedback and bug reports.** You see the whole report before you save or copy it. A report contains your answers, the game version, the date and a random report ID. Each of the following is included only if you switch it on for that report: settings and the run's seed, controls and preferences, your high score and last-run score, usage totals (how long and how you play), frame-rate totals, and a rough description of your device (processor type, graphics family, Android version). If you link your reports, they share a random ID created by the game, not your name or a device identifier.

These exports are copies outside the game. Uninstalling or deleting data in the game does not delete them, and the game cannot recall them. Text you copy can stay in your clipboard history or be synced to your other devices, depending on your phone's settings. Whoever you send an export to receives what it contains.

## Feedback reports and support emails

Feedback report files are part of a pilot open only to invited adult testers. A report reaches me only if you email the file yourself to moonshine-run@proton.me, after agreeing in the game that I may process it. I use reports only to fix bugs and improve the game, based on your consent. Raw reports and their emails are deleted within 30 days of receipt, and the totals taken from them within 90 days. Reports are never sold, published or used for advertising.

Anyone can email me for support. Sending an email shows me your email address and whatever you write. I use support emails only to answer you, and delete them once your question is resolved, at the latest six months after the last message.

Email is delivered and stored by Proton AG (Switzerland). Reports may also be stored on my own access-restricted computer.

## Children

Moonshine Run is not made for children under 13, and I do not knowingly receive their data. Feedback reports are accepted only from invited adults.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its use, or a copy of it in a portable format, and you can object to its use, where these rights apply. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before. Email moonshine-run@proton.me with the subject "Privacy request". I reply without undue delay, normally within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party fonts. GitHub, as the host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the page and keep it secure; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, I update this page before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
