# Moonshine Run

A portrait car-chase game: haul moonshine down country roads and outrun the law. Coming to Google Play; iPhone later.

## Support

For questions, bug reports or feedback, email **moonshine-run@proton.me**. For a bug, please say which phone you use and what happened just before it.

## Privacy

The game has no internet access and sends nothing. Your scores, settings and progress stay on your phone. Read the [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}).
